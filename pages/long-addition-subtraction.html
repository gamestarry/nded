Long Addition With Carrying

In long addition, begin in the ones column and move left. For 478 + 256, first add 8 + 6. The answer is 14, so write 4 in the ones place.

8 + 6 = 14 7 + 5 = 12, then add the carry of 1 4 + 2 = 6, then add the carry of 1

When a column makes a two-digit total, the Tutor shows the carry as its own next step. First calculate the two digits in the current column; if the previous column made a carry, handle that carry separately when the Tutor asks. This keeps place value clear and helps students see why 478 + 256 = 734.

The Tutor does not jump straight to the final answer. It guides you through each addition and carry so you can do the thinking.

Long Subtraction With Borrowing

In long subtraction, start at the ones column. When the top digit is too small to subtract the bottom digit, choose Borrow 1. Borrowing is also called regrouping, but this Tutor uses the clear action name Borrow 1.

After you borrow, the current column receives ten more ones. The column that lent the ten has a small −1 above it. Finish the current column first. When you move left, the Tutor asks you to deal with that −1 before you do the normal subtraction in the new column.

This one-column-at-a-time order makes each change visible. It is especially useful when a zero appears in a subtraction problem.

Subtraction With Borrowing Across Zeros

Try 1204 − 38. Write 38 as 0038 so the places line up. The ones column, 4 − 8, needs a borrow. Choose Borrow 1, finish 14 − 8 = 6, and leave the small −1 above the tens column.

The answer is 1166. The Tutor does not treat this as one big borrow from the thousands place to the ones place. Instead, it shows the borrowing work one column at a time.

Tips for Addition and Subtraction Practice

  • Line up digits by place value before you start.
  • Begin with the ones column and move left one column at a time.
  • In addition, watch for a carry after you finish the two digits in a column.
  • In subtraction, use Borrow 1 as soon as the top digit is too small.
  • Use Show Me One Step if you are stuck, then take the next step yourself.
  • Check the completed problem and explain one carry or borrow in your own words.

FAQ

Does this tutor show every step?

It guides students through every needed column, including carries in addition and borrowing steps in subtraction. Students still enter the work themselves.

What is carrying in addition?

Carrying happens when a column total is 10 or more. You write the ones digit in the current column and use the remaining ten in the next column.

What is borrowing in subtraction?

Borrowing means taking one ten, hundred, or larger place from the column to the left so the current column has enough to subtract.

Is borrowing the same as regrouping?

Yes. Both words describe changing a value into equivalent place-value parts. This Tutor calls the action Borrow 1.

Can it handle borrowing across zeros?

Yes. The Tutor handles it one column at a time, including the small −1 that must be handled in the next column.

How many digits can I enter?

Each number can have up to eight digits.

What does Show Me One Step do?

It completes only the current step. It does not solve the entire problem for you.

Are decimals or negative numbers supported?

No. This version supports non-negative whole numbers only; decimals and negative numbers are not supported.
