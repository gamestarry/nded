FAQ

How many long division problems can I practice?

You can use New Problem as many times as you want during the session.

Can I practice with remainders?

Yes. Choose With Remainders before you generate a problem.

Can I practice with 2-digit divisors?

Yes. Choose 2-Digit Divisor to practice divisors from 10 to 99.

Does the practice tool show the full solution?

No. It checks your answer. Use the calculator link if you need the full work.

What should I do if I get an answer wrong?

Try again, or use Show Answer when you are ready to compare.
